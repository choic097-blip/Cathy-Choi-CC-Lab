[Mushroom Kingdom Oracle]

Summon a power-up for your soul — a Mario-themed divination tool

01. Concept Proposal

The Idea — What if a Mario question block could give you life advice? The Mushroom Kingdom Oracle is a small ritual: type a worry you're carrying, strike the block, and receive a "power-up" — a randomly summoned Mario character paired with one of three energy types (Fire Flower, Star, or Mushroom) and a short oracle message that reframes your situation in Mario's logic.

Why Mario — The Mario universe has its own emotional grammar. A fire flower is power. A star is a fleeting window of invincibility. A mushroom is recovery — getting bigger, getting your second chance. These aren't just game mechanics; they map surprisingly well onto real emotional states. The oracle uses that mapping to deliver advice that feels playful, not preachy.

How It Works — The user types their worry into a textarea. The script reads the text and looks for emotional keywords — "tired," "stuck," "alone" suggest a Mushroom (recovery) reading; "scared," "afraid," "doubt" suggest a Fire Flower (power) reading; ambiguous or short text defaults to Star (momentum). Once the energy type is chosen, the script fetches a random character from the public Amiibo API and pairs it with a hand-written oracle message from the matching energy bucket.

Visual Direction — Old-school NES aesthetic. Press Start 2P for headers, VT323 for body text, the iconic Mario sky-blue gradient with floating clouds, and a 3D question block that bounces in place until you click it. The block has the chunky pixel-art shadows and inset highlights that signal "this is interactable" without needing a label.

Stack — Single-file HTML, CSS, Vanilla JavaScript. Live API call to amiiboapi.org for Mario character data. No frameworks, no build step.
